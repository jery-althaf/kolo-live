## Key Messaging Rules

✅ **DO:**
- Use specific numbers (1000+, ₹1.2L, 4.9/5)
- Focus on outcomes (save money, avoid mistakes)
- Speak to fears (getting ripped off, quality issues)
- Use active voice ("We help you save")
- Include social proof in every section

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use jargon ("synergistic solutions")
- Be vague ("high quality", "best service")
- Make unbelievable claims ("Save 90%!")
- Hide pricing (be transparent)
- Overwhelm with options (max 3 packages)

## Conversion Copywriting Formulas

### PAS Formula (Problem-Agitate-Solve)

**Problem:**
"You've saved for years to build your dream home."

**Agitate:**
"But one wrong contractor, one inflated quote, one design
mistake—and you could lose lakhs of rupees or end up with
a house you hate."

**Solve:**
"Kolo's expert advisors ensure you make the right decisions
at every step—from choosing professionals to final delivery."

---

### FAB Formula (Features-Advantages-Benefits)

**Feature:**
"Quote Analysis Report"

**Advantage:**
"Line-by-line breakdown showing fair prices vs. markups"

**Benefit:**
"You save ₹30,000-₹1,00,000 by paying only fair rates"
